################ THE DURATION CASE, AND A NOTE ON 8e4062f ################
  "how long does it take to get from 45 to 99 mining at granite rates?"
  was the flaky case in the --repeat 3 run above. xp_between declined it,
  the model answered it twice out of three and refused it once.

    --only granite --repeat 5     before  2/3 (of 3)   ~13s a run
                                  after   5/5           ~3.8s a run

  It answers by looking up the rate the asker pointed at:

    Mining 45 to 99 is 12,972,919 XP. At 126,000 XP/hour that is 103.0 hours.
    Pay-to-play Mining training, Levels 45-99: Granite: "The table uses an
    efficient long-term benchmark of 126,000 experience per hour."

  The sentence is quoted because that section states four rates -- 134,000
  tick-perfect, a 126,000 long-term benchmark, 120,000-125,000 for players
  making occasional mistakes, 63,000 without tick manipulation. They are
  not four opinions about one number, and an answer that gives the figure
  without saying which one it is has dropped the half that decides whether
  it applies to the asker.

  Three conditions, each of which was a wrong answer first:

    * the method must be NAMED. Twelve rates overlap 45-99 in that guide,
      and picking one unprompted is the same act as inventing it.
    * the method matches on the HEADING. Four brackets mention granite in
      their prose -- iron ore says to switch to it at 45 -- and one is
      about it.
    * the bracket must COVER the whole range. A rate stated for 15-70 does
      not describe 45 to 99, and pairing the full gap with it contradicts
      itself in the way consistent_hours_from exists to catch.

  Full run with it in, before the pronoun fix:

    26/28 cases passed every run  (82/84 answers overall)
    flaky: cannonballs 2/3, jewellery 2/3 -- both model-answered, both
    wobbling run to run, neither touched by the router.
